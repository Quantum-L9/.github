# Quantum-L9/.github

> Semantic Foundation v3.5.0 for the Quantum-L9 organization. `semantics/` is the canonical language for product topology, kinds, identity, architecture law, and compilation. Domain repositories own domain meaning. This repository also runs the organization governance, control, and distribution plane, and that plane does not own product meaning.

Do not copy `semantics/` into a product repository. Cite `artifact_id`. The compiler carries out recorded decisions. It does not invent what a product means. SDK is not an admitted ProductKind. Canonical CI is `Quantum-L9/l9-ci-core`.

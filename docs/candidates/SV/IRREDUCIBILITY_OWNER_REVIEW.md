## Review points on the amended `L9-SV-001`

1. **Narrowed consequence clause.** The original said unknown or incompatible inputs "cannot establish a complete Strategic Value ranking". The amended text says unknown, incompatible or materially unidentified inputs "cannot establish a complete **causally attributed** Strategic Value ranking". Read literally, a ranking that does not claim causal attribution is no longer covered by that sentence. The first sentence still requires compatible units and a declared baseline for any quantified claim. The owner should decide whether the narrowing is intended or whether "causally attributed" should be removed.
2. **Divergence wording.** The original SV-015 required that divergence between intervention and baseline be explicitly *quantified*; the amended SV-001 requires it to be explicitly *identified and justified under the selected method*. The verbatim SV-015 statement is preserved in the lineage record, and the contract obligation is unchanged, but the global-law wording is looser.

## Admission question and semantic gap

The admitted term `strategic_value` denotes **quantified** objective-relative expected net utility, and `strategic_value_assessment` denotes a **quantitative** derived assessment. The existing `L9-SV-001/002` trigger is a quantified SV claim. A consumer also needs a non-numerical, source-bound *screen* for an unquantified direction or potential, without impersonating a completed quantitative claim. A different claim, *realized compounding*, needs evidence of later beneficial consumption, not just forecast, connectivity, stored information, or recorded delivery. These two gaps are narrower than the original 19 proposed rules.

**Scope:** These are conditional rules for a consumer **choosing to make** the named qualitative or realized-compounding claim. They do not compel every task to assert Strategic Value, do not rank mandatory safety/security/compliance work by a multiplier, and do not weaken hard constraints.

### Semantic tests that separate the candidates from admitted law

| Candidate | Valid example and expected interpretation | Counterexample that MUST fail | Why existing law alone is insufficient |
|---|---|---|---|
| `L9-SVQ-001` | Agent describes plausible reuse benefits and maintenance risk with declared evidence and a feasible baseline; reports **conditional** qualitative potential, without a number | Agent calls graph degree or five anecdotes a verified 85-point SV ranking, or hides a harmed consumer | `L9-SV-001/002` and the SV assessment contract trigger on **quantified** claims; general evidence law does not specify this distinct qualitative-claim boundary |
| `L9-SVQ-002` | A new reusable artifact is actually consumed in a later authorized job; an additional net beneficial outcome is observed and material countervailing effects are examined | Agent claims a stored lesson, mirrored packet, unchanged feedback loop, or planned reuse is already compounded realized value | The admitted quantitative model addresses forecast/realization and time-indexed causal accounting, but not this unquantified, later-consumption proof threshold |

**No semantic substitution:** `L9-SVQ-001` is not a weaker version of `L9-SV-001/002`. An assessment asserting a numerical SV value must satisfy **all** presently applicable quantitative rules and contracts regardless of whether the qualitative screen ran first. A qualitative screen may be informative without being a prerequisite for a quantitative one.

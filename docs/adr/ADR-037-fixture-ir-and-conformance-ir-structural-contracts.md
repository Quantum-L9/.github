## Context

`semantics/compilation_profiles.yaml` (`l9.compilation/product-build@1`) defines Stage 8 (`fixtures`), which produces `fixture_ir` under `fixture-derivation-validator/v1`, and Stage 12 (`conformance`), which consumes `fixture_ir` and produces `conformance_ir` under `reference-conformance/v1`. `semantics/ir_catalog.yaml` names both IRs and the `law → fixture → conformance` transitions. `semantics/conformance_model.yaml` fixes the required shape of a conformance requirement and forbids a fixture inventing law, the system under test defining its expected result, and unresolved semantics compiling to a false pass. `semantics/binding_catalog.yaml#pytest/v1` maps `fixture_case`, `given`, `when.operation` and `expect`.

At `Quantum-L9/.github@f8e44d1536cb218de41dde4685c606a4d6460915` (as at `dcd9d00`, where this record was first drafted), nothing states what an `l9.fixture-ir/v1` or an `l9.conformance-ir/v1` contains, so both required validations have nothing to check against. A compiler that defined the shapes itself would own fixture and conformance semantics, which `authority_model.yaml#compiler_authority.may_not` forbids.

`l9.law-ir/v1`, Stage 8's declared law input, has no structural schema either, and Stage 7 (`laws`) is not realized.

## Options considered

- **A — Let the compiler define the shapes.** No Foundation change; the compiler becomes the semantic owner. Rejected.
- **B — An inline grammar in `ir_catalog.yaml`.** No enforcement reads it. Rejected.
- **C — Two `l9.schema-definition/v1` artifact schemas in the existing mechanism (the ADR-027 precedent).** Enforced by the existing interpreter and resolver; cross-field obligations become named `semantic_rules` for the registered stage solvers.

## Decisions (proposed)

Option C.

1. `semantics/fixture_ir.schema.yaml` (`l9.schema/fixture-ir@1`, const `l9.fixture-ir/v1`) and `semantics/conformance_ir.schema.yaml` (`l9.schema/conformance-ir@1`, const `l9.conformance-ir/v1`) are listed under `generic_compiler_manifest.yaml#requires.artifact_schemas`. Both are governed by contracts, not by a projection or composition profile.
2. **Fixture case:** `id`; `disposition` (`executable` | `unresolved`); non-empty `source_law_refs`; `given.steps[]` of declared contract-typed setup (`interface_action` or `port_fixture`); `when` as exactly one `operation` + `request` (`mode: single`) or a declared `sequence` / `parallel` group; `expect` over when-step results, the group and declared `read_observation` operations only; `provenance`. Expected values are literals traced to a cited law; `$observed` is relational only and never self-referential; `$from` names only earlier steps.
3. **Conformance obligation:** the `conformance_requirement_shape` of `conformance_model.yaml` bound to the fixture cases that witness it, with `disposition`, a target-neutral `execution_plan`, and verdicts `pass | fail | error | unresolved`. An obligation with an unresolved witness or no executable witness is `unresolved` and never yields `pass`.
4. **Interim law input.** Until an owner-admitted `l9.law-ir/v1` schema exists, Stage 8 takes its law input from `law_index`: governed, digest-bound semantics already in the product's resolved closure (`product_invariant`, `admitted_contract`, `admitted_invariant`) with `source {artifact, revision, digest}`. Every `source_law_refs` entry resolves to exactly one entry, and `provenance.law_index_digest` binds the index. This is an explicit substitution for Stage 8's `semantic_law_ir` and `law_provenance` inputs, stated in the schema's `interim_law_input` block. It is part of the admission subject and is not canonical until admitted. It defines no law-ir shape; a successor `l9.schema/fixture-ir@2` replaces it once a law-ir schema is admitted.
5. **Fixture profile.** The conformance IR records `fixture_profile.status`. When the product declares no applicable profile, it records exactly one typed `fixture_profile_absent` Unknown and no obligation carries a `profile_hint`; absence is never a satisfied requirement, and product-declared cases still compile.
6. **Validator.** `scripts/validate-semantics.py` RC-035 checks registration and the successor surfaces (RC-035-NEG: 20 mutations fail closed) and runs `scripts/fixture_conformance_ir.py` over `ops/fixtures/fixture-conformance-ir/`: 6 positive witness files (fixture IR, conformance IR, harness surface, a synthetic manifest for the closure digest, product files with a RepositorySpec slice, and reference evaluations including the outcomes that hold) and 111 falsifying fixtures, at least one per checked, evaluated or cross-document rule. Rules that need a resolved manifest closure, the product repository's code, or state a non-authority property remain the stage solvers' obligations.
7. **Harness surface and invocation context** (operator decision, relayed by the coordinator). The fixture IR's `interface_surface` declares `forbidden_actions`, `harness_controls` (`clock`, `journal_retention`, `fault_injection`, `authorization_policy`, `internal_setup`; only `fault_injection` and `internal_setup` may name a `target_port_ref`) and `harness_observations`. A given step of kind `harness_control` names a declared control; a fault-injection request is `{port_method, code, after_effect: before | after}`. `internal_setup` (for example a retention executor's hard erase) is never a Gate action and never a when-operation, and a forbidden interface action stays forbidden. Every Gate action step may carry a `context {tenant_ref, principal_ref, authorization_ref?, budget_ms?}` of fixture literals. Observations carry `kind: interface_action | harness_observation`. Seven rules are added, each with a falsifying fixture.
7a. **Value references** (coordinator direction within decision 7, from State's audit of 69 cases). `$observed` relational operands (`equals`, `not_equals`, `greater_than`, `less_than`) may name any given step, when step (including a sibling of the same parallel group) or observation of the case, because assertions are evaluated after every step ran; they never name their own target value or an unknown step. A target or `$observed` path absent from the recorded result makes the assertion an error, never a pass (proven by the reference evaluator `evaluate_relational`). A harness control step is a `$from` source only when its control declares a `result_contract_ref` (for example State's retention-executor erase result); a harness observation is never a `$from` source. Falsifiers: f37, f38, f39, e01, e02.
8. **Product-declared fixture cases** (operator decision). `product_topology.schema.yaml#properties.conformance` gains two optional fields: `fixture_case_refs: [{ref, digest}]` (product-owned `l9.fixture-ir/v1` documents, not fixture profiles, so the self-issued-profile prohibition is untouched) and `harness_surface_ref`. `conformance.required` stays `[profile_refs]`; existing topologies stay valid. `l9.compilation/product-build@1` stage 8 consumes `product_fixture_cases`; `vocabulary.yaml#semantic_build_stages` carries the same input (RC-013 stage parity).
9. **`pytest/v2`** (operator decision F1-D2). `binding_catalog.yaml` registers `pytest/v2`, `successor_of: pytest/v1`, mapping groups, observations, harness controls and observations, context and unresolved cases. `pytest/v1` is unchanged (RC-035 pins its canonical-JSON digest `cbff6899…34d1`).
10. **Grading instrument.** This release is the first graded by the admission skill's IR-schema registration rule (commit `c85ca1b`, operator decision F1-D1 relayed by the coordinator): a profile-less `l9.schema-definition/v1` whose const is an `ir_catalog.yaml` IR id registers through defined `governed_by.contracts`.
11. No solver, conformance-model, invariant, contract, term or profile change is part of this release. Ledger changes are limited to `binding_catalog.yaml` (`pytest/v2`), `compilation_profiles.yaml` and `vocabulary.yaml` (stage 8 inputs), `product_topology.schema.yaml` (optional conformance fields, product-file resolution and six rules), `product_manifest.schema.yaml` (optional closure digest and compilation record, two rules) and `ir_catalog.yaml` (one entry, decision 15).

### Amendment of 2026-11-12 (operator decisions "Approve package" and D-OBLIGATIONS = "Derive from law", relayed by the coordinator)

12. **Closure binding (U-B).** The fixture IR and the conformance IR bind `subject.closure_digest` / `provenance.closure_digest`, never `manifest_digest`: Stage 8 runs before Stage 13 writes the manifest digest. `product_manifest.schema.yaml#closure_digest_definition` defines it exactly as the semantic digest of the manifest without `manifest_digest`, `closure_digest` and `conformance.compilation`. A manifest that records `conformance.compilation {fixture_ir_digest, conformance_ir_digest}` records `closure_digest` too, and both digests stay in the manifest. Both new manifest fields are optional; `required` is unchanged.
13. **Digest bases (U-A).** A product-authored file (a fixture case document, the harness surface) is bound by the sha256 of its raw bytes; a derived IR or index by its semantic digest (`fixture_ir.schema.yaml#digest_bases`). `harness_surface_ref` becomes `{ref, digest}` and stays optional.
14. **Product file resolution (D-FIXREF).** A product file ref resolves to exactly one RepositorySpec `source_surfaces.authoritative` entry whose `coordinate` equals it, at a path confined to the repository. Zero or several matches, an escaping path and a digest mismatch are typed refusals (`product_file_ref_unresolved`, `product_file_ref_ambiguous`, `product_file_path_escapes_repository`, `product_file_digest_mismatch`), never a resolution.
15. **Harness surface schema (U-C).** `semantics/harness_surface.schema.yaml` (`l9.schema/harness-surface@1`, const `l9.harness-surface/v1`): envelope `{schema, product_ref, owner, revision?}`; controls and observations reuse the fixture-IR entry shapes and add a required `implementation_ref` (verified, never regenerated, never a Gate action). It registers through the IR-schema route of decision 10, as directed; that route requires the const to be an `ir_catalog.yaml` id, so `l9.harness-surface/v1` is added there although the document is product-authored input rather than a compiler-derived IR. Stage 8 consumes `product_harness_surface` (`compilation_profiles.yaml` and `vocabulary.yaml`, RC-013 parity). This answers the stage-8 harness-input question recorded at drafting time.
16. **Presence semantics (F1-D6).** `is_present` holds iff the path resolves, `is_absent` iff it does not, `is_null` iff it resolves to null; an absent path is never an error for them. Only a relational predicate whose target or `$observed` path is absent is an error. The 1d3255e rule is renamed and scoped accordingly.
17. **`count_where`.** The operand is `{where: [{path, predicate, operand?}], count}`: a non-empty conjunction of scalar clauses (no `result_contract_is`, literal operands only) and an exact non-negative count. The 1d3255e `{path, equals, count}` form is retired and the exemplars are migrated.
18. **Precondition.** A given step's `precondition` is a flat `{dotted path: literal}` equality map; one that does not hold is a setup error, never a fail or a pass.
19. **Observation context.** An `interface_action` observation may carry the Gate invocation context, under the same shape and literal-only rules as a step's; a harness observation never does.
20. **Obligations derived from law (D-OBLIGATIONS).** The conformance IR has exactly one obligation per `law_index` entry: `governing_refs = [law_ref]`, `subject_class` = the law's `law_kind`, `observable_semantics` = the law's `statement`, `verification_class` = the law's `verification_class`, `witnessed_by` = exactly the cases that cite it. `law_index` entries therefore carry `statement` and `verification_class`. `verification_class` is a closed domain (`fixture_replay`, `static_inspection`, `external_runtime_evidence`); only `fixture_replay` can be executable, and any other class is unresolved, carries no execution plan, and never passes. This closes U4.

## Review findings (recorded, not resolved)

- **`pytest/v1` alignment.** Single-operation cases align exactly with `pytest/v1`; every other form is mapped by `pytest/v2`.
- **Unknowns** (`docs/candidates/R5-F1/README.md`): U0 the `l9.law-ir/v1` schema (owner-owned follow-up). U1–U3 are closed by decision 7, U4 by decision 20, and the stage-8 harness-input question by decision 15. RepositorySpec authoritative source entries do not yet carry the `coordinate` that decision 14 reads; that schema is owned outside this repository.
- **Mechanism choices made in the amendment** (reported for the owner, not separate decisions): the law's `statement` and `verification_class` are declared on the `law_index` entry, because an obligation is derived from it and a class is never defaulted; `subject_class` is the law's `law_kind`; `count_where` clauses take literal operands only; a fixture-replay obligation with witnesses carries an execution plan even while unresolved.
- **Language test.** Five State cases (`Quantum-L9/l9-wip@ce0c4973`) encode with no State-specific field and all are executable; `expired_event_cursor` follows State's settled C5 (page form) and reaches its precondition through a declared `journal_retention` control. State's harness refs in that file are stand-ins until State declares its harness surface.
- **Rehearsal.** The compiler agent rehearsed the 1d3255e candidate under a test-only lock; this amendment folds in its findings and the operator's package, and the compiler re-locks to the amended candidate. That rehearsal is separate evidence.

## Consequences

- Stages 8 and 12 have canonical structures to produce and validate. A compiler cannot change their shape without contradicting this admitted contract.
- A fixture case without cited law, with an expectation taken from the system under test, or an unresolved obligation reported as passing, is invalid before any target rendering.
- Every law in a product's `law_index` yields exactly one obligation; a law that fixture replay cannot prove is recorded as unresolved, never as a pass.
- A product's harness surface and fixture cases are bound by raw-bytes digest and resolved only through its RepositorySpec; an unresolvable or tampered product file is a typed refusal.
- Admission of this candidate grants no producer, compiler or consumer any mutation, publication, merge, deployment or execution authority.
